ABC iView changelog

1.0.0 - 2026-07-30
- First packaged release under the plugin.video.abc_iviewjc add-on ID.
- Added ABC Account QR-code/device-code linking.
- Added My Watchlist.
- Added Continue Watching.
- Added live ABC channel browsing and playback.
- Renamed the live-channel menu entry to Watch Live to distinguish it from
  ABC's separate ABC TV editorial catalogue section.
- Updated category, collection, series and episode handling for current ABC
  catalogue responses.
- Added README, GPLv3 licence, metadata, disclaimer and release documentation.
